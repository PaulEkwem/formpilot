FAQ

Questions officers ask first

Is my customer's data really not stored anywhere?

Correct. The PDF is generated entirely inside the customer's browser using client-side JavaScript. No BVN, no ID number, no address, no field value reaches our servers. We only know that a link was opened and completed — never what was typed into it.

Does the customer need to download an app?

No. They open the link in any web browser — Chrome on Android, Safari on iPhone, anything. The form runs in the browser; the PDF downloads to their device when they finish.

What if the customer doesn't finish in one sitting?

Their progress auto-saves on their device as they go. They can close the tab and come back later via the same link to continue where they left off.

What if the customer doesn't have email?

You can send the link via WhatsApp directly from your dashboard with one click — most Nigerian customers prefer that anyway.

Which banks and forms are supported today?

GTBank's Banker's Reference Form (Individual + Corporate) and Sole Proprietorship / Partnership account-opening form are live. Corporate, Trustees, Societies and KYC are next on the roadmap. Other banks (Access, Zenith, First, UBA) are added based on community demand.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. There is no contract and no minimum term — cancel any time from your dashboard, no questions asked.
